# Content pillars

Five pillars, each tied to personas and an existing blog category (`blog/categories.json`). Where a post is history, set the `tag` to History and use the closest category (a separate `history` category could be added to `blog/categories.json` first, with its Cantonese name, if the history posts grow).

| # | Pillar | Days | Posts | Main personas | Blog categories | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fly the Approach | Mon, Fri | 26 | Sim Sam, Casual Casey | flying, controls, scores | Feature, Update |
| 2 | Real Hong Kong 1998 | Tue, Sat | 25 | Wing, History Harry, Casual Casey | scenery | Feature, History |
| 3 | Aircraft Hangar | Wed | 13 | Sim Sam, History Harry | aircraft | Feature, History |
| 4 | The Kai Tak Story | Thu | 13 | History Harry, Wing, Eddie | flying, scenery, aircraft | History |
| 5 | Behind the Build and Community | Sun | 13 | Eddie, Sim Sam | site, scores | Behind the scenes, Update |

## 1. Fly the Approach (Mon and Fri)
**Promise:** you will land at Kai Tak, and land it better.
- **Mondays, learn:** a step-by-step curriculum from the first approach to autoland, weather, night, take-offs and Runway 31.
- **Fridays, challenge:** scoring, ranks, controls, shareable weekend challenges, replay videos.
- **Facts come from:** README (IGS 13, heading 088, 47° right turn, ranks), `docs/highscores.md`, `docs/replays.md`.
- **Call to action:** fly it now, post your report card.

## 2. Real Hong Kong 1998 (Tue and Sat)
**Promise:** see Hong Kong as it was.
- **Tuesdays, the place:** Kowloon City, the harbour, estates, traffic, skyline, neon, built from open data.
- **Saturdays, the view:** Plane Spotter, the Peak, the helicopter tour, the harbour, the control tower.
- **Facts come from:** README (data sources, corrections for 1998), `docs/helicopters.md`.
- **Call to action:** open Plane Spotter or the helicopter tour; share the post with someone who grew up there (Cantonese version first for this pillar).

## 3. Aircraft Hangar (Wed)
**Promise:** know every aircraft and why it flies the way it does.
- One post per type or system: 747-400, 777-200, A330/A340, Concorde, An-225, cabins, flight decks, control surfaces, cargo, ground handling, sound.
- **Facts come from:** README, `docs/concorde.md`, `docs/an225.md`.
- **Rules:** no real airline names or logos; fictional airlines only.
- **Call to action:** fly this aircraft.

## 4. The Kai Tak Story (Thu)
**Promise:** why Kai Tak was one of the most remarkable airports in the world.
- History and context: the geography, the approach, the checkerboard, air traffic control, a busy apron, Flight 605, typhoon season.
- **Facts come from:** README, `docs/`, and sources named in the post. Anything not already in the repo gets a source link before it is published.
- **Call to action:** fly the approach and see it for yourself.

## 5. Behind the Build and Community (Sun)
**Promise:** how this sim gets made, and what players are doing with it.
- Build logs (how the map is made, how the tests work), data and sources, the Cantonese version, stats, roadmap, round-ups, community feedback.
- **Facts come from:** repo, `docs/stats.md`, `docs/analytics.md`, commit history.
- **Call to action:** send feedback, follow on Facebook and Instagram, share the site.

## Content types
| Type | Length | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Guide | 600 to 900 words, 3 to 4 screenshots | Pillar 1, 3 |
| Photo story | 400 to 600 words, 4 to 6 screenshots | Pillar 2 |
| History explainer | 600 to 900 words, sources named | Pillar 4 |
| Build log | 400 to 700 words | Pillar 5 |
| Challenge | 300 to 500 words, one replay video | Friday posts |
