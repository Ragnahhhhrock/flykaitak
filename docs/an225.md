# Antonov An-225 Mriya (premium aircraft)

A flyable six-engine An-225, US$7 one-off, sold and unlocked exactly like Concorde (see `docs/concorde.md`). Product id `an225`, licence keys `MRYA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`, free giveaway codes `FKT-XXXX-XXXX` made with `"product":"an225"` in `POST /admin/codes`. Generic green livery: no real airline names or logos.

## What is different
- **Model** (`AC.an225`, `buildAircraftModel`): high swept wing with anhedral (`hw`, `dih`), three engines a side, twin endplate fins on the stabiliser (`twin`), no windows (`noWin`), two twin-wheel nose legs and seven twin-wheel legs a side in fairings (32 wheels).
- **Flight model**: 88.4 m span, 905 m² wing, 1,377 kN thrust, Vref 148 kt, slow roll (10°/s), flaps 0/15/25/35/40. Wingtip strike is worked out from the high, drooping wing (`a.tipY`).
- **Take-off weight** (`mTO` 560 t, `mTOwet` 520 t when the weather rain is above 0.3; maximum is 640 t): Kai Tak's 3,390 m runway on a hot July day. A toast says so on free flight. Fuel burn 12 kg/s at full thrust (`burn`).
- **Taxiways** (`rwyOnly`): the 88 m wings do not fit them. On the ground the An-225 must keep within 26 m of the centreline: at speed that is a runway excursion, at walking pace it stops at the edge.
- **Traffic**: departures hold six seconds further back from the runway for the An-225 (`rcHolds`), so a held jet is clear of its wings.
