**About / Story:**
Fly Kai Tak is a browser flight simulator of the 1998 Runway 13 IGS approach into Hong Kong Kai Tak.

Descend over Tsing Yi on the instrument guidance system, fly toward the checkerboard on the hill at Kowloon Tsai, make the 47° right turn low over Kowloon City, and land on a runway that ends in Kowloon Bay. Every landing is graded out of 100. Every crash tells you why.

Built on real Hong Kong data: Lands Department aerial imagery, about 24,000 building footprints, and SRTM terrain. Kowloon City shop signs, Nathan Road neon, public housing estates, harbour traffic, the terminal and apron, weather from clear to typhoon, day and night, ATC radio, and more.

No download. No sign-up. Play at flykaitak.com.
