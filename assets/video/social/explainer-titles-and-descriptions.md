# Fly Kai Tak · 60-second explainer: titles and descriptions

`flykaitak-explainer-60s.mp4` · 1920×1080, 16:9, 24 fps, 56 seconds, with an original synthesised soundtrack (`tools/reel_music.py`), so there is nothing to license.

The shots come from the sim itself: the reel footage in this folder, plus live captures of the real interface (cockpit decks, Plane Spotter, the landing lesson, the helicopter tour, and the replay with its Save video dialog). Nothing from inside the passenger cabin.

## What it shows
1. The IGS 13 approach and the 47° right turn over Kowloon City
2. Real Hong Kong in 1998: about 24,000 buildings, about 6,900 vehicles
3. Weather and time of day: typhoon, storm, rain at night
4. Four airliners (747-400, 777-200, A330-300, A340-300) with working cockpits
5. Modes: Plane Spotter with the live flight board, the landing lesson, the helicopter tour
6. **How to watch and save a flight highlights reel**
   - Every flight is recorded; when it ends the highlights play by themselves
   - The Director camera cuts between chase, fly-by and cockpit shots
   - Tap a marker (checkerboard, turn, touchdown) to jump to it
   - Save video renders an MP4 in your browser: Landscape 16:9 (YouTube, X, Facebook) or Portrait 9:16 (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)
   - Download the MP4, Share…, or Copy caption
