# Fly Kai Tak · social reel: titles and descriptions

One cut of about a minute, recorded from the sim itself (cockpit deck off, no overlays, nothing from inside the passenger cabin). The music is an original track composed and synthesised for the reel (`tools/reel_music.py`), 144 BPM in E minor and cut to the picture, so there is nothing to license and no copyright claim to expect. On Instagram you can swap it for the app's audio when you post.

**Description**
```
Hit the checkerboard. Turn right.

Fly Kai Tak is a free flight simulator that runs in your browser: the 1998 Runway 13 approach into Hong Kong's Kai Tak airport. Everything in this video was recorded from the simulator itself.

In this reel:
• A 747-400 on final at night
• An A330 breaking out of low cloud over Kowloon
• The 47° right turn low over Kowloon City rooftops
• Jets passing just overhead of a Kowloon City street
• Touchdowns in clear air, in rain at night, in a typhoon crosswind (777-200) and in a storm (A340)

How it works: descend over Tsing Yi on the instrument guidance system, fly toward the checkerboard on the hill at Kowloon Tsai, make the 47° right turn, and land on a runway that ends in Kowloon Bay. Every landing is graded out of 100, and every crash tells you why.

Built on real Hong Kong data: aerial imagery, about 24,000 building footprints, the 1998 road network and SRTM terrain. Day and night, clear skies to typhoon.
```
