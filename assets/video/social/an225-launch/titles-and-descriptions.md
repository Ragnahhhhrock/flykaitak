# Fly Kai Tak · An-225 Mriya launch promo: titles, descriptions, tags

Two cuts of the same promo (about 27 seconds each, 24 fps), recorded from the sim itself (cockpit deck off, overlays hidden; `tools/an225_promo.py`). Seven takes: the final over Kowloon, the checkerboard turn, low over the rooftops, touchdown on Runway 13, the wingtips past the runway edge, a capped-weight take-off, and a night landing. Each take has one on-screen line; the video ends on a "Fly the An-225." card with flykaitak.com. Music is the project's own synthesised track, so nothing to license. The livery is the sim's generic green: no real airline names or logos. The real An-225 never flew to Kai Tak; this is a what-if.
