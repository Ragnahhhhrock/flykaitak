# Fly Kai Tak · Concorde launch promo: titles, descriptions, tags

Two cuts of the same promo, **about 26 seconds each, 24 fps**, recorded from the sim itself (cockpit deck off, overlays hidden; `tools/concorde_promo.py`). Six takes: the final over Kowloon, the checkerboard turn, low over the rooftops, touchdown on Runway 13, a full-reheat take-off, and a night landing. Each take has one on-screen line, and the video ends on a "Fly Concorde." card with flykaitak.com. The music is the project's own synthesised track (`tools/reel_music.py`), so there is nothing to license. The livery is the sim's generic navy and red: no real airline names or logos.

## Rebuild

```
python3 tools/concorde_promo.py scout yt            # 3 stills per take, to check framing
python3 tools/concorde_promo.py render yt           # then: render ig   (frames are resumable; about an hour in software WebGL)
python3 tools/concorde_promo.py build               # overlays, end card, music -> this folder
python3 tools/concorde_promo.py image               # the two still images -> this folder
```
