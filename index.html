IGS approach · Runway 13

Hit the checkerboard, turn right, land before the harbour.

You start the descent over Tsing Yi at about 3,000 ft, established on the instrument guidance system, heading 088. Follow it toward the orange-and-white checkerboard on the hill, then make the 47° right turn low over Kowloon City and line up with a runway that ends in Kowloon Bay.
