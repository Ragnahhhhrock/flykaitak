Fly Concorde at Kai Tak

Concorde is now a flyable aircraft: a one-off US$7 unlock, or free with a giveaway code.

Concorde is now a flyable aircraft. It is a one-off US$7 unlock, and giveaway codes can unlock it for free.

Flying it

  • Slender ogival delta wing, four engines in paired pods, tall fin, drooping nose. The nose lowers with the gear and rises when it is up.
  • A delta-wing flight model: it lands nose-high and fast, with no flaps. Take-off uses reheat, with its own sound and flames.
  • A flight engineer panel in the cockpit. The livery is generic navy and red: no real airline names or logos.
  • The autopilot flies the Runway 13 IGS approach and lands it on both runways in every weather preset.

Unlocking it

Choose Concorde on the setup screen. Pay once through Stripe Checkout and you get a licence key (CONC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) that is saved in your browser. Type the key into the code box on another device to restore it.

Got a code?

Giveaway codes (FKT-XXXX-XXXX) go in the same box and unlock Concorde for free. Each code has a limited number of uses.

協和式客機而家可以親自飛喇。一次過解鎖 US$7，亦可以用贈送代碼免費解鎖。

飛行特色

  • 修長嘅三角翼、成對引擎吊艙、高高嘅垂直尾翼同可下垂嘅機鼻。放起落架機鼻就下垂，收起落架就升返起。
  • 三角翼飛行模型：機頭高、速度快咁降落，冇襟翼。起飛用復燃，有專屬聲音同火焰。
  • 駕駛艙有飛行工程師面板。塗裝係通用嘅深藍同紅色，冇用真實航空公司名稱或標誌。
  • 自動駕駛可以飛 13 跑道 IGS 進場，兩條跑道、所有天氣都降到落地。

點樣解鎖

喺設定畫面揀協和式客機，用 Stripe 付款一次，就會有一個授權碼（CONC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX），儲喺瀏覽器入面。喺另一部裝置將授權碼輸入代碼欄就可以還原。

有代碼？

贈送代碼（FKT-XXXX-XXXX）輸入同一個欄位，就可以免費解鎖協和式客機。每個代碼有使用次數限制。
