Landmarks tour, plus Ocean Park and Happy Valley

From the Bank of China Tower to the Garden Hill beacon: the landmarks you can spot around Kai Tak, and two new additions, Ocean Park and Happy Valley Racecourse.

Fly Kai Tak is full of places you can recognise from the cockpit, the tower or a spectator spot. Here is a tour, now with Ocean Park and Happy Valley Racecourse added.

Central and Wan Chai

Look across the harbour for the Bank of China Tower, Central Plaza, The Center, HSBC, Jardine House and Exchange Square. The Convention Centre extension sits on the Wan Chai waterfront.

Tsim Sha Tsui and Hung Hom

On the Kowloon side are the TST Clock Tower, the Cultural Centre, Ocean Terminal and the Hung Hom Coliseum.

Kai Tak and Garden Hill

The Kai Tak terminal and tower are on the apron. In Sham Shui Po, the Garden Hill obstacle beacon marks the hill under the approach, and at night it flashes red.

New: Happy Valley Racecourse

The racecourse sits in its bowl among the Happy Valley towers, with sixteen floodlight masts, the stand canopy and the infield screen. At night the masts light up.

New: Ocean Park

Ocean Park is built at its real site on the south side. The Waterfront has a Ferris wheel. On the Summit are the Ocean Park Tower, a roller coaster in the style of The Dragon, and a Space Wheel. Red cable cars run between the two, with pylons along the way.

Heads up

Ocean Park and Happy Valley are stylised models placed at the real sites, not exact copies. They are solid, so you can crash into them.

中環同灣仔

望過海對面，會見到中銀大廈、中環廣場、中環中心、滙豐總行、怡和大廈同交易廣場。會議展覽中心新翼喺灣仔海旁。

尖沙咀同紅磡

九龍一邊有尖沙咀鐘樓、文化中心、海運大廈同紅磡體育館。

啟德同嘉頓山

啟德客運大樓同控制塔喺停機坪上。深水埗嘅嘉頓山障礙物燈標示進場航道下面嘅山丘，夜晚會閃紅光。

新增：跑馬地馬場

馬場坐落喺跑馬地高樓之間嘅碗狀地形入面，有十六支泛光燈柱、看台頂篷同場中央螢幕。夜晚燈柱會亮起。

新增：海洋公園

海洋公園建於南區真實嘅位置。海濱樂園有一個摩天輪。山上樂園有海洋摩天塔、仿照過山龍風格嘅過山車，同一個太空摩天輪。紅色纜車往返兩地之間，沿途有支柱。

注意

海洋公園同跑馬地係喺真實位置擺放嘅風格化模型，並唔係準確複製。佢哋係實心嘅，所以你可以撞上去。
