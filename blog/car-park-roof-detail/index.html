More detail on the car park roof

Globe lamps, a pipe railing along the parapet and a stairwell head with its exit and caution signs.

The Kai Tak car park roof was the spotters' favourite spot, and in the Plane Spotter view it now looks like the photos: white globe lamps on black posts, a pipe railing along the parapet, and a stairwell head with its signs.

Globe lamps

Lamp standards stand in rows across the deck: two staggered rows on the runway side (the nearer one about 7 m in from the parapet) and one along the far edge. Each is a black post with a white globe, and the globes light up at night.

Parapet and railing

The roof now has a proper parapet wall about 0.7 m high with a pipe rail and a mid rail on posts above it. A grey crowd barrier stands 2.2 m inside it on the runway side, with a yellow line painted on the deck beside it. The deck also has parking bays on the far side and lane dashes down the middle.

Stairwell entry and signs

At the end of the roof there is a white stairwell block with a flat slab roof, a steel door, a green exit sign above it and a yellow caution board beside it, with a wall light that comes on at night.

圓球燈

天台上面一行行咁企住燈柱：跑道嗰邊有兩行錯開咗，近嗰行距離欄河大約 7 米，另外遠邊緣仲有一行。每支都係黑色燈柱加白色圓球，夜晚球會亮。

欄河同扶手

天台而家有條真正嘅欄河牆，大約 0.7 米高，上面有鋼管扶手同中間橫桿。跑道嗰邊向內 2.2 米位置企住一排灰色人群欄障，旁邊地面畫咗條黃線。地面遠邊仲有停車位，中間有車道虛線。

樓梯間入口同標誌

天台一端有個白色樓梯間，平頂、鋼門，門上面有綠色出口牌，旁邊有塊黃色小心牌，夜晚仲有盞牆燈會亮。
