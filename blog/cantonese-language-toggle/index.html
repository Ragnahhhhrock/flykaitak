Fly Kai Tak in Cantonese: a language toggle on the home screen

A new EN / 粵 button on the home screen switches all of the setup text between English and Cantonese, and remembers your choice.

3 October 20262 min readFly Kai Tak

The home screen now has a language button in the top left, next to Blog. Press 粵 to read every line of the setup screen in Cantonese, and EN to switch back.

EN / 粵One buttontop left, beside Blog
RememberedStays seton your next visit
Whole screenAll the textmodes, aircraft, weather, controls

What changes

The headline and intro, the mode, aircraft, time and weather choices, the option tick boxes, the Begin descent button and its notes, the Controls list, the data credits and the Contact line all switch to Cantonese. The 啟德機場 title already on the screen stays as it is. Aircraft names appear as 波音 (Boeing) and 空中巴士 (Airbus).

Notes

  • The text is written Cantonese, for example 喺 and 嘅, rather than formal written Chinese.
  • Your choice is saved in your browser, so the page opens in the language you last used.
  • This covers the home screen. The in-flight buttons, the result screen and the ATC radio are still in English.
  • Flying is unchanged: the autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still crashes.
