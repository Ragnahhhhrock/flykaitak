Plane Spotter: watch Kai Tak from the car park roof

No flying needed: pick a viewpoint, watch the traffic and use the sim clock to see the whole day.

Plane Spotter is the relaxed way into Fly Kai Tak. You do not fly. You choose a viewpoint, watch the traffic come and go, and use the sim clock to see more of the day. The car park roof beside the runway end is a good place to start, with the plane-spotters already there.

The car park roof

The roof of the Kai Tak car park looks over the terminal to the apron, the runway and, beyond them, Hong Kong Island across the harbour.

What to watch

  • Arrivals and departures on the runway, one movement at a time, with go-arounds when the runway is not clear.
  • Turnarounds at the stands: arrivals dock at the jet bridges, and a departure waits, retracts the bridge and is pushed back by a tug.
  • Concorde, which visits Plane Spotter for free.

Controls that help

  • Zoom 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× and 16× with I and O.
  • Time 1×, 2×, 4× and 8× with T, and jump back or forward ten seconds with [ and ].
  • M shows the live map with a dot where you are watching, and B shows the flight information board.

Other viewpoints: the Peak, the top of Checkerboard Hill, a Kowloon City street, Prince Edward Road beside the threshold, and a junk moored in the harbour.

停車場天台

啟德停車場嘅天台可以望過客運大樓，見到停機坪、跑道，再遠啲係海港對面嘅香港島。

睇啲咩

  • 跑道上嘅升降：一次只有一架使用跑道；跑道未清空嗰陣，降落嘅飛機會復飛。
  • 停機位周轉：抵達嘅飛機靠上空橋，離港嘅飛機等候、收回空橋，然後由拖車推出。
  • 協和式：佢會免費到訪飛機迷模式。

有用嘅操控

  • 用 I 同 O 放大縮小：1×、2×、4×、8×、16×。
  • 用 T 轉時間速度 1×、2×、4×、8×，用 [ 同 ] 後退或前進十秒。
  • M 顯示即時地圖，仲有一點標示你嘅觀察位置；B 顯示航班資訊板。

其他觀景點：太平山、棋盤山頂、九龍城街道、跑道頭旁邊嘅太子道，同泊喺海港嘅一艘帆船。
