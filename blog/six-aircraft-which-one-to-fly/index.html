Six aircraft at Kai Tak: which one should you fly?

Four free airliners, Concorde and the An-225: their approach speeds, flight decks and how they differ.

Fly Kai Tak has six aircraft you can fly: four airliners that are free, and two premium aircraft. Each one has its own flight model (mass, wing area, thrust, approach speed, roll rate, flap labels and limits), so they do not all feel the same on the IGS 13 approach.

The four everyday airliners

  • Boeing 747-400: four engines, Vref 150 kt, span 64.4 m. A CRT glass flight deck with yokes and four thrust levers. Flaps UP, 1, 10, 20 and 30.
  • Boeing 777-200: two engines, Vref 140 kt, span 60.9 m. An LCD glass flight deck with yokes. Flaps UP, 1, 5, 20 and 30.
  • Airbus A330-300: two engines, Vref 138 kt, span 60.3 m. Sidesticks, an ECAM display and the blue-grey Airbus deck. Flaps 0, 1, 2, 3 and FULL.
  • Airbus A340-300: four engines, Vref 142 kt, span 60.3 m, with the same Airbus deck and flap settings as the A330.

The two premium aircraft

Concorde and the Antonov An-225 are US$7 each. Concorde has a delta wing with a span of 25.6 m, no flaps, and the highest approach speed of the six at 162 kt. It also visits Plane Spotter for free. The An-225 has six engines and 88.4 m wings, Vref 148 kt. Its take-off weight is capped for Kai Tak's runway, and because its wings do not fit the taxiways it stays on the runway on the ground.

Which one to pick

  • First flight: the 747-400. It is the default, and the landing lesson is written around it.
  • A slower landing: the A330-300, which has the lowest approach speed of the six at 138 kt.
  • A different challenge: Concorde, which has no flaps and the highest approach speed, or the An-225, whose roll rate setting (10) is the lowest of the six, against 14 to 18 for the others.

Every aircraft is flown the same way: pick it on the start screen, choose IGS 13 and fly toward the checkerboard. Your score is graded the same for all six.

Fly Kai Tak 有六款飛機任你揀：四款免費客機，加兩款付費飛機。每款都有自己嘅飛行模型（重量、翼面積、推力、進場速度、滾轉率、襟翼標示同限速），所以喺 IGS 13 進場嘅手感唔會一樣。

四款常用客機

  • 波音 747-400：四部引擎，進場速度 150 節，翼展 64.4 米。CRT 玻璃駕駛艙，配駕駛盤同四支推力桿。襟翼 UP、1、10、20、30。
  • 波音 777-200：兩部引擎，進場速度 140 節，翼展 60.9 米。LCD 玻璃駕駛艙，配駕駛盤。襟翼 UP、1、5、20、30。
  • 空中巴士 A330-300：兩部引擎，進場速度 138 節，翼展 60.3 米。側桿、ECAM 顯示同藍灰色嘅空中巴士駕駛艙。襟翼 0、1、2、3、FULL。
  • 空中巴士 A340-300：四部引擎，進場速度 142 節，翼展 60.3 米，駕駛艙同襟翼設定同 A330 一樣。

兩款付費飛機

協和式同安托諾夫 An-225 每款 7 美元。協和式係三角翼，翼展 25.6 米，冇襟翼，進場速度 162 節，係六款之中最高。佢仲會免費出現喺飛機迷模式。An-225 有六部引擎、88.4 米翼展，進場速度 148 節；起飛重量為咗啟德跑道而設上限，而且翼展太闊，滑行道放唔落，所以喺地面只可以留喺跑道上。

點樣揀

  • 第一次飛：747-400。佢係預設，降落課程都係圍繞佢設計。
  • 想慢啲著陸：A330-300，進場速度 138 節，六款之中最低。
  • 想試吓唔同挑戰：協和式冇襟翼、進場速度最高；An-225 嘅滾轉率設定係 10，六款之中最低，其他款係 14 至 18。

六款飛機嘅飛法一樣：喺開始畫面揀飛機，揀 IGS 13，然後向棋盤飛。評分標準對六款都一樣。
