The car park roof view moves to the new car park

The Plane Spotter car park view now stands at the rail of the new landside car park, looking over the terminal to the runway.

5 October 20261 min readFly Kai Tak

The Car park roof view now puts you on the new car park, on the landside of the terminal. You stand at the parapet rail on the road-side corner of the runway end of the low roof, with the crowd of spotters behind and beside you.

A clear view over the terminal

The car park is now a little taller (eight decks on the low section, nine on the high one), so from the rail you look over the terminal roof to the stands, the runway and the harbour, and out over Kowloon City to the IGS turn. The spotters no longer stand in the camera's line of sight.

Try this

Start Plane Spotter and choose Car park roof from the Plane spotter menu, or press C until it comes up.

「停車場天台」視角而家會帶你去客運大樓陸側嘅新停車場。你企喺矮段天台跑道嗰頭、近馬路嗰個角嘅欄河邊，觀機人群喺你後面同旁邊。

望過客運大樓冇遮擋

停車場而家高咗少少（矮段八層、高段九層），所以喺欄河邊可以望過客運大樓頂，睇到停機位、跑道同海港，仲可以望過九龍城睇 IGS 轉彎。觀機人群唔會再擋住鏡頭。

試吓

開始 觀機，喺觀機位置選單揀 停車場天台，或者一直撳 C 直到見到佢。
