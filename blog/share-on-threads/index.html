Every blog post now has a Threads share button alongside X, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email and copy link.

Every blog post now has a Threads share button, next to X, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

How it works

The button opens a new Threads post with the article title and link filled in, ready for you to edit and publish. Clicks are counted as a share event with the method threads.

每篇網誌文章而家都有 Threads 分享掣，同 X、Facebook、Reddit、LinkedIn 同 WhatsApp 放埋一齊。

點運作

呢個掣會打開一個新嘅 Threads 帖文，已經填好文章標題同連結，由你編輯同發佈。每次㩒掣都會記錄為 share 事件，方法係 threads。
