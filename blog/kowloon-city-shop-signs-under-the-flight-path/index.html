Kowloon City in 1998: the shop signs under the flight path

Period shop signs, rooftop aerials and scaffolding, and a jet passing just overhead.

Look up from a Kowloon City street in 1998 and a jet passes just overhead. Look at the street itself and it is a wall of signs. Fly Kai Tak rebuilds that street scene from period photos, so the approach you fly has the shops, signs, aerials and scaffolding that sat under it.

The signs

The Kowloon City shop signs come from period photos. They include 新澧傢俬, 鳳香園, 金輝粥麵專家, 珍珍珠寶金行, 君皇酒樓 and 黃珍珍, among others. Signs hang across the streets, and there are vertical and flat boards along the buildings. Real corporate logos are left out.

Above the shops

The street scene goes up to the roofs: rooftop billboard frames, air-conditioners, laundry poles and bamboo scaffolding, and a forest of aerials.

See it yourself

  • Pick the Kowloon City street spectator view and wait: the aircraft passes just overhead.
  • Or fly the IGS 13 approach and look down as you make the turn.

On Nathan Road, projecting neon signs glow at night with a halo, and giant billboards for eight invented brands sit on the rooftops. None of them use real logos.

招牌

九龍城嘅商舖招牌係參考當年相片，包括新澧傢俬、鳳香園、金輝粥麵專家、珍珍珠寶金行、君皇酒樓同黃珍珍等等。街上有橫跨馬路嘅招牌，樓宇外牆有直招牌同平招牌。真實嘅企業標誌就冇用。

商舖之上

街景一路延伸到天台：天台廣告牌框架、冷氣機、晾衫竹同竹棚架，仲有一大片天線。

自己嚟睇

  • 揀九龍城街道觀景視角，等一陣：客機會貼住頭頂飛過。
  • 或者飛 IGS 13 進場，轉彎嗰陣向下望。

彌敦道嘅伸出式霓虹招牌喺夜晚會發光，帶光暈；天台有八個虛構品牌嘅巨型廣告牌。全部都冇用真實標誌。
