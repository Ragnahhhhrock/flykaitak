Redrawn aircraft: embedded cockpits and real winglets

The cockpit glass is cut into the fuselage, the winglets are swept blades and the wings taper, on all four aircraft.

All four aircraft in the sim have been redrawn. The flight deck windows are cut into the fuselage instead of sitting on top of the nose, the winglets are swept and tapered instead of square plates, and the wings now thin out toward the tip.

A330 and A340 winglet2.1 m, blended24° cant, curved root

The flight deck

Before, the cockpit glass was a dark slab standing on the nose, tilted up and clear of the fuselage. The fuselage is now built as one smooth skin from nose to tail, and the windscreen, the side windows and the cabin window row are all cut into it. They follow the curve of the nose and the taper of the tail. The 747-400 also gets a shaped upper deck with its own windscreen and window row.

The winglets

The 747-400 winglet is now a swept, tapered blade canted 29° from vertical. The A330-300 and A340-300 use a slightly taller winglet that curves up out of the wing in a blend, canted 24°. The 777-200 keeps its plain wingtip.

Wings, engines and the rest

  • Wings are lofted from their outline with a flat top and an underside that is deepest near the front. Thickness follows the chord, so the root is deep and the tip is thin.
  • Engines have a lipped intake, a fan cowl and a smaller core cowl. Each hangs from a swept pylon that runs up into the wing, in place of the old box.
  • Cabin windows are individual windows. The cheatline follows the fuselage all the way into the tail cone.
  • The nose droops a little and the tail cone sweeps up, with the shape set for each aircraft.

What did not change

  • The flight model. Weights, speeds and sizes are the same.
  • The autopilot still lands all four aircraft in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.

駕駛艙

之前駕駛艙玻璃係一塊黑色嘅板，企喺機鼻上面，向上傾斜，同機身分開。而家機身係由機鼻到機尾一張連貫嘅表面，擋風玻璃、側面窗同客艙窗全部開喺度，跟住機鼻嘅弧度同機尾嘅收窄。747-400 仲有一個有形狀嘅上層，自己有擋風玻璃同一排窗。

翼尖小翼

747-400 嘅小翼而家係一塊向後掠、愈近尖端愈窄嘅葉片，傾斜 29°。A330-300 同 A340-300 用較高少少嘅小翼，由機翼圓滑咁彎起，傾斜 24°。777-200 保持普通嘅翼尖。

機翼、引擎同其他部分

  • 機翼按外形砌出嚟，上面平坦，下面近前面最深。厚度跟住翼弦，所以翼根深、翼尖薄。
  • 引擎有唇形入口、風扇罩同較細嘅核心罩，由一條後掠嘅吊架連去機翼，取代咗之前嘅四方盒。
  • 客艙窗係一個個獨立嘅窗。腰線會沿住機身一直去到機尾錐。
  • 機鼻稍為向下，機尾錐向上翹，形狀按每款飛機分別設定。

冇改嘅嘢

  • 飛行模型。重量、速度同尺寸都冇變。
  • 自動駕駛仍然可以喺所有天氣設定下降落四款飛機，完全唔操作仍然會墜毀。
