Every hand-flown IGS 13 landing in Fly Kai Tak is scored out of 100. The score is built from eleven parts, each one a smooth curve: full marks only inside a tight window, then a straight-line fall to nothing outside it. A 100 needs every part inside its window on the same flight.

The eleven parts

  • Sink rate at touchdown (16): 120 fpm or less for full marks, zero at 480 fpm.
  • Touchdown point (14): 280 to 450 m past the threshold; zero at 100 m or 900 m.
  • Glidepath (14): 6 m RMS or less from 3.5 nm out to the flare; zero at 40 m.
  • Centreline at touchdown (8): within 1.5 m; zero at 12 m.
  • Touchdown speed (8): within 2 kt of Vref plus 5; zero at 14 kt.
  • Approach track (8): 14 m RMS or less; zero at 70 m.
  • Speed control (8): 2.5 kt RMS or less; zero at 12 kt.
  • Rollout centreline (8): never more than 3 m off; zero at 14 m.
  • Stopping distance (7): 800 m of runway left; zero at 150 m.
  • Alignment (5): within 1.5° of runway heading; zero at 8°.
  • Wings level (4): under 1° of bank; zero at 5°.

A tail strike takes 15 points off, and the score is rounded down, so a 100 means every part was full.

Ranks

Your score sets your rank: Captain at 90 and up, First Officer at 78, Second Officer at 62, Flight Engineer at 45, and Cadet below that.

How to lose fewer points

  • Look at the two parts the result screen says lost the most, and work on those first.
  • The approach parts (glidepath, track and speed control) are only scored on the IGS approach, so a steady final matters from 3.5 nm out.
  • Aim to arrive at Vref plus 5 kt over the threshold.

Not ranked: autopilot landings, landings after a rewind, free flight, Plane Spotter and crashes. Lesson landings are not ranked either.
