The real road network, as it was in 1998

Traffic now drives the Transport Department's surveyed road centrelines, with every road built after 1998 taken out.

The traffic now drives the Transport Department's own road centrelines instead of lines traced off a map picture. Roads that were built after 1998 have been taken out, so the network is the one you would have flown over in the last year of Kai Tak.

What changed

  • Accurate roads: every main road follows the surveyed centreline of each carriageway, joined through the junctions into long runs.
  • No tunnels on the hills: tunnel sections are left out, so traffic no longer drives over Lion Rock or across the harbour on the water.
  • Kowloon City streets: the streets under the approach come from the same data, which also places the shop signs and the asphalt there.
  • Street lights: spaced a little wider, since each carriageway now has its own row.

Taken out for 1998

  • Route 8 (Tsing Sha Highway and Stonecutters Bridge, 2008 to 2009).
  • Tsing Yi North Coastal Road and Hung Hom Bypass (1999).
  • Central-Wan Chai Bypass and the roads on the Central Reclamation Phase III and Tamar waterfront.
  • Everything on the Kai Tak site: the new streets on the old apron and runway.
  • The West Kowloon Cultural District streets, Cyberport Road, and streets first recorded after 2009, such as the Anderson Road estates.

Source: Road Network Data of Hong Kong (Transport Department Intelligent Road Network, File Geodatabase by Esri China (HK)). Rebuild it with python3 tools/roads.py.

而家啲車行嘅係運輸署自己嘅道路中線，唔再係由地圖圖片描出嚟嘅線。1998 年之後先起嘅路已經拎走，所以個路網就係啟德最後一年你飛過嘅嗰個。

改咗乜

  • 準確嘅路：每條主要道路都跟住每條行車道實測嘅中線，喺路口接駁成長段。
  • 山上面冇隧道：隧道段拎走咗，啲車唔會再喺獅子山上面或者喺海面行。
  • 九龍城街道：進場航道下面嘅街道都係用同一份數據，招牌同柏油路面都係跟佢擺。
  • 街燈：間距闊咗少少，因為而家每條行車道都有自己一行燈。

為咗 1998 拎走嘅路

  • 8 號幹線（青沙公路同昂船洲大橋，2008 至 2009 年）。
  • 青衣北岸公路同紅磡繞道（1999 年）。
  • 中環灣仔繞道，同埋中區填海第三期同添馬海旁嘅路。
  • 啟德舊址上面所有嘢：舊停機坪同跑道上面嘅新街。
  • 西九文化區嘅街、數碼港道，同埋 2009 年之後先有紀錄嘅街，例如安達臣道嘅屋苑。

資料來源：Road Network Data of Hong Kong（運輸署智能道路網絡，Esri China (HK) 製作嘅 File Geodatabase）。用 python3 tools/roads.py 重新建立。
