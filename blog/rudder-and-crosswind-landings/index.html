A rudder for crosswind landings

Kick out the crab before touchdown with the new rudder, try the Crosswind weather, or let auto-rudder do it.

Fly Kai Tak now has a rudder. In a crosswind the jet still flies crabbed, with the nose pointed into the wind, but now you have to straighten it just before touchdown. The landing score counts the crab, and there is a new Crosswind weather preset to practise in.

Why the jet crabs

An airliner flies through the air, and the air itself is moving. To track straight down the runway line in a crosswind, the nose has to point a little into the wind. That angle is the crab. In the Crosswind preset it is about 7° on short final, and more in the gusts. The flight path marker on the HUD shows where you are really going, and a new readout under the heading box shows the crab, for example CRAB 7° L: the nose is 7° left of your track.

Kick it straight

Landing crabbed drags the tyres sideways. Just before touchdown, press the rudder toward the runway to swing the nose into line: . for right rudder, , for left. On a phone or with a mouse, drag the new Rudder bar above the yoke; it springs back to the centre when you let go. A full pedal holds about 11° of sideslip at approach speed. The bar under the heading box on the HUD shows where the pedals are.

The rudder does more than turn the nose:

  • It slowly swings the flight path the same way, so press it late, in the last few seconds, or the jet drifts downwind.
  • It rolls the jet a little toward the yawed side, so hold a touch of opposite aileron to keep the wings level.
  • It adds drag.

On the runway

After touchdown the pedals steer the nosewheel. The arrow keys still work too. A crosswind now pushes the nose into the wind as you roll out, so expect to hold a little rudder until you slow down.

A crabbed touchdown is graded. Under 2.5° earns the full 10 points, under 5° earns 7 and under 8° earns 3. More than that earns nothing and a note on the result screen. Land more than 15° off your direction of travel, for example by kicking the pedal the wrong way, and the main gear gives way. The other items were rebalanced so the total is still 100, and the result screen and report card now show Crab at touchdown.

Crosswind weather and auto-rudder

The new Crosswind preset blows from 050 at 32 knots, gusting 46, almost straight across Runway 13. It is dry with good visibility, so you can watch the crab and the runway at the same time. For a Runway 31 approach the wind swings to the opposite quarter, as with the other presets. Typhoon is still the harder test, with rain and low cloud on top.

If you would rather not use the pedals, tick Auto-rudder in Options: the computer straightens the nose over the last 40 ft and keeps it straight on the runway. It is always on in the take-off and landing lessons. The autopilot de-crabs in the flare as well, and lands in every weather preset on both runways.

Technique: stay crabbed all the way down final and let the flight path marker sit on the runway. At about 30 ft, press rudder toward the runway until the nose lines up, hold the wings level and touch down.

點解飛機會蟹行

客機係喺空氣入面飛，而空氣本身都喺度郁。要喺側風入面沿住跑道中線直飛，機頭就要稍為向住風嚟嘅方向，呢個角度就係蟹行角。喺「側風」天氣，短五邊大約係 7°，陣風時仲大。HUD 上嘅飛行路徑標記會顯示你真正飛去邊度；航向框下面新加咗一個讀數顯示蟹行角，例如 CRAB 7° L，即係機頭喺你嘅航跡左邊 7°。

一腳踩直

帶住蟹行角落地，輪胎會被打橫拖。接地前一刻，向跑道方向踩方向舵，將機頭擺返正：. 係右舵，, 係左舵。用手機或者滑鼠，就拖動操縱桿上面新加嘅 Rudder 方向舵條，放手就會彈返中間。進場速度下，踩盡方向舵大約可以維持 11° 側滑。HUD 航向框下面嗰條橫條會顯示踏板位置。

方向舵唔止會轉機頭：

  • 佢會慢慢將飛行路徑都推向同一邊，所以要遲啲先踩，喺最後幾秒先踩，否則飛機會向下風飄。
  • 佢會令飛機向偏擺嗰邊少少側傾，所以要反方向帶少少副翼，保持機翼水平。
  • 佢會增加阻力。

喺跑道上

接地之後，踏板會控制前輪轉向，方向鍵照樣用得。而家側風會喺滑行減速時將機頭推向風嚟嘅方向，所以減速之前要預咗一路帶住少少方向舵。

帶住蟹行角接地會被評分：少過 2.5° 攞足 10 分，少過 5° 有 7 分，少過 8° 有 3 分，再大就零分，結果畫面仲會有提示。如果接地時機頭同行進方向相差超過 15°，例如踩錯邊，主起落架就會頂唔住。其他評分項目重新分配過，總分仍然係 100，結果畫面同成績表而家會顯示 Crab at touchdown（接地蟹行角）。

側風天氣同自動方向舵

新嘅側風天氣由 050 吹嚟，風速 32 節，陣風 46 節，幾乎係正正打橫吹過 13 號跑道。天氣乾爽、能見度好，你可以同時睇住蟹行角同跑道。飛 31 號跑道進場時，風向會轉去相反方向，同其他天氣一樣。颱風仲係更難嘅考驗，因為仲有雨同低雲。

如果唔想自己踩踏板，可以喺 Options 剔選 Auto-rudder（自動方向舵）：電腦會喺最後 40 呎將機頭擺正，喺跑道上保持直行。起飛同降落課程一定會開。自動駕駛喺拉平時都會自己消除蟹行角，喺兩條跑道、所有天氣都可以降落。

技巧：成個五邊都保持蟹行，等飛行路徑標記壓住跑道。大約 30 呎時，向跑道方向踩方向舵，等機頭對正，保持機翼水平，然後接地。
