The Kai Tak shop is open

Tees, a cap, a patch, a pin, a poster, a mug and stickers, all from the checkerboard and the IGS 13 approach. Linked from the home screen.

Fly Kai Tak now has a shop at flykaitak.com/shop: eight pieces of original merch built from the sim's own design system, the checkerboard, the IGS 13 approach and 1998 Kai Tak.

What's in it

  • Checkerboard tee (A$39): black, with the Kowloon Tsai checkerboard and 啟德 on the chest.
  • IGS 13 approach tee (A$39): the 088° course, the 47° right turn and Runway 13, as a chart.
  • Runway 13 cap (A$32): jade green, with the embroidered checkerboard mark.
  • Kai Tak 1998 patch (A$12) and checkerboard enamel pin (A$14).
  • Turn right at the checkerboard poster (A2, A$35), a final approach mug (A$24) and a sticker sheet (A$9).

Every design is original and uses no real airline names or logos.

How ordering works

Coming soon

The first run is being made. Each product has a Notify me button that emails contact@flykaitak.com; we will reply when it is ready to order. No payment is taken yet. When ordering opens, the button becomes Buy and goes to a secure Stripe checkout.

On the home screen

There is a new Shop button next to Blog at the top of the home screen, and a Kai Tak merch card under the coffee button. The blog header links to the shop too. Every order helps keep the sim free to fly.

Fly Kai Tak 而家有商店，喺 flykaitak.com/shop：八件原創精品，全部用返模擬器自己嘅設計：棋盤、IGS 13 進場，同 1998 年嘅啟德。

有啲咩

  • 棋盤 T 恤（A$39）：黑色，心口印九龍仔棋盤同「啟德」。
  • IGS 13 進場 T 恤（A$39）：088° 航道、47° 右轉同 13 號跑道，畫成航圖。
  • 13 號跑道帽（A$32）：翡翠綠，繡上棋盤標誌。
  • 啟德 1998 布章（A$12）同棋盤琺瑯襟章（A$14）。
  • 棋盤右轉海報（A2，A$35）、最後進場杯（A$24）同貼紙套裝（A$9）。

所有設計都係原創，冇用任何真實航空公司嘅名或標誌。

點樣訂

即將推出

第一批貨製作緊。每件貨品都有有貨通知我掣，會電郵去 contact@flykaitak.com；有得訂嘅時候我哋會回覆你。暫時唔會收錢。開賣之後，個掣會變成購買，帶你去安全嘅 Stripe 結帳頁面。

主畫面

主畫面頂部「網誌」旁邊多咗個商店掣，咖啡掣下面亦有張啟德精品卡。網誌頁頂亦有連結去商店。每張訂單都幫手令模擬器繼續免費。
