New: giant billboards and neon over Kowloon

Floodlit hoardings on the airport road and glowing neon signs, visible from the IGS at night.

Hong Kong in the 1990s was lit by its advertising. Kai Tak now has giant floodlit billboards on the rooftops and roadsides under the approach, and building-sized neon signs that glow at night.

Billboards on the airport road

Large hoardings stand on rooftops and on poles beside the roads around Kowloon City, Kowloon Bay and the approach. They face the road or the incoming aircraft, have steel frames and catwalks, and are lit by rows of floodlights at night. They are kept under the approach clearance, so none sits in your flight path.

Neon

Three rooftop signs are drawn as glass tubes: a watch face, a red electronics wordmark and a restaurant and night club sign. By day they are pale, unlit tubes. At night they glow with a bright core and a halo. The projecting shop signs on Nathan Road and the other main roads are bigger, more of them are neon, and they glow too.

No real logos

The brands are made up: an electronics maker, a soft drink, a whisky, western-style jeans and boots, colour film, a car, a watch and a night club. They use the era's styles but no real logos, like the rest of the signs in the sim.

機場路上嘅廣告牌

九龍城、九龍灣同進場航線附近嘅天台同路邊，企住大型廣告牌。佢哋面向馬路或者進場嘅飛機，有鋼架同維修通道，夜晚由一排排泛光燈照住。廣告牌高度會控制喺進場安全高度之下，唔會阻住你嘅航線。

霓虹

三個天台招牌用玻璃管畫成：一個錶面、一個紅色電器字牌，同一個酒家夜總會招牌。日間佢哋係淺色、未亮嘅燈管。夜晚就會發出明亮嘅光芒同光暈。彌敦道同其他主要馬路嘅伸出式招牌亦都更大、霓虹更多，同樣會發光。

冇真實商標

品牌全部係虛構嘅：電器、汽水、威士忌、西部風牛仔褲同靴、菲林、汽車、鐘錶同夜總會。用咗當年嘅風格，但同模擬器入面其他招牌一樣，唔會用真實商標。
