Blog categories, search and pages

Browse the blog by category, sort by category, page through 20 posts at a time and search every post.

The blog now has categories, a sort by category view, pages of 20 posts and a search box.

Categories

Every post belongs to one category: Aircraft, Flying & Procedures, Scenery & Weather, Controls & Devices, Scores & Sharing, or Site & Language. The category shows on each post card and at the top of each post. Pick a category chip on the blog to see only those posts.

Sort and pages

Choose Newest or By category above the posts. Each page shows 20 posts, with Previous, Next and page numbers underneath.

Search

Type in the search box to find posts by title, description, keywords, category or the words in the post. Results appear as you type, 20 to a page, and work in English and Cantonese.

網誌而家有分類、按分類排序、每頁 20 篇同搜尋框。

分類

每篇文章都屬於一個分類：飛機、飛行同程序、風景同天氣、操控同裝置、分數同分享，或者網站同語言。分類會顯示喺每張文章卡同每篇文章頂部。喺網誌㩒一個分類掣，就只會睇到嗰類文章。

排序同分頁

喺文章上面揀最新或者按分類。每頁顯示 20 篇，下面有上一頁、下一頁同頁碼。

搜尋

喺搜尋框輸入文字，就可以按標題、簡介、關鍵字、分類或者文章內文搵文章。結果會一邊打字一邊更新，每頁 20 篇，英文同廣東話都搵得到。
