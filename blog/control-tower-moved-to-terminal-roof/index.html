The control tower is back on the terminal roof

Kai Tak's control tower now stands on the passenger terminal at its runway end, where it really was, with its outward-leaning cab windows.

The Kai Tak control tower now stands where it really was: on the roof of the passenger terminal, at its runway end, where the pier meets the taxiway. It used to be a separate tower on an island in the apron.

What is new

  • The tower rises off the terminal roof: a plant storey with a band of windows, a dark storey set back beneath the cab, then the cab itself.
  • The cab glass leans outward, wider at the top than at the bottom, so controllers could look straight down onto the stands. The plan is a rectangle with cut corners, as in period photos.
  • A balcony rail runs round the foot of the cab, and a deep flat roof with a dark fascia overhangs the leaning glass. A red beacon and two masts sit on top.
  • Satellite dishes and aerial masts stand on the terminal roof beside the tower.
  • From the cab you look straight down onto the taxiway and Runway 13/31, with the harbour beyond, and along the pier and apron toward Lion Rock, as in period photos.
  • At night the cab glows, as before.

What did not change

  • The tower is solid: fly into it and the flight ends.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.

啟德控制塔而家企返喺佢真正嘅位置：客運大樓天台近跑道嗰頭，即係碼頭接滑行道嘅位置。以前佢係停機坪中間一座獨立嘅塔。

有咩新

  • 控制塔由大樓天台升起：一層有一排窗嘅機房，喺塔頂下面有一層縮入去嘅深色樓層，再上面就係塔頂控制室。
  • 控制室嘅玻璃向外斜，上闊下窄，等管制員可以直接望落停機位。平面係切咗角嘅長方形，同舊相一樣。
  • 控制室底部有一圈露台欄杆，上面有個深色邊嘅平頂，伸出遮住斜玻璃。頂上有一盞紅色燈同兩支天線。
  • 塔旁邊嘅大樓天台有衛星碟同天線杆。
  • 由控制室可以直接望落滑行道同 13/31 跑道，後面係海港；另一邊沿住碼頭同停機坪望向獅子山，同舊相一樣。
  • 夜晚控制室會好似以前咁發光。

冇變嘅嘢

  • 控制塔係實心嘅：撞埋去就會墜機。
  • 自動駕駛喺所有天氣設定下照樣降落，完全唔操作照樣會墜機。
