A tidier home screen

Fewer panels over the scenery: the flight board starts off, one latest update, and follow, share and coffee move to the foot of the menu.

11 October 20262 min readFly Kai Tak

The home screen had grown a lot of extras over the past weeks: a demo reel, the flight information board, five blog posts, a merch card, a coffee button, follow links and seven share buttons, all stacked over the live view of Kai Tak. It is now trimmed back to what matters when you arrive: the name, the newest update, and the menu to pick a flight.

What changed

  • Flight board off by default. The split-flap arrivals and departures board no longer opens on its own. Press B or the Flights button at the top to show it; your choice is remembered.
  • One latest update. The panel now shows the newest blog post with its card image, plus All updates on the blog.
  • No demo reel. The live sim already runs behind the menu, so the video inset went.
  • Shop in the top bar only. The separate merch card is gone; Shop is still at the top.
  • Follow, share and coffee moved down. Facebook, Instagram, Buy me a coffee and a single Share button now sit at the bottom of the menu, under Controls. Share opens the full list: X, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp, Threads, email and copy link.

過去幾個禮拜，主畫面加咗好多嘢：示範片、航班資料板、五篇網誌、精品卡、請飲咖啡掣、追蹤連結同七個分享掣，全部疊喺啟德嘅即時畫面上面。而家精簡返，淨係留低一入嚟最重要嘅：名、最新消息，同揀航班嘅選單。

改咗啲乜

  • 航班資料板預設關閉。翻牌式到港同離港資料板唔會自己彈出嚟。㩒 B 或者頂部嘅 Flights 掣就會顯示，會記住你嘅選擇。
  • 只顯示最新一篇。面板而家只顯示最新一篇網誌同佢嘅卡片圖，加埋「網誌睇晒所有更新」。
  • 冇咗示範片。選單後面本身已經行緊即時模擬，所以唔使再放片。
  • 商店只喺頂部。獨立嘅精品卡拎走咗，頂部仲有 Shop。
  • 追蹤、分享同咖啡搬咗落去。Facebook、Instagram、請飲咖啡同一個 Share 掣而家喺選單最底，Controls 下面。㩒 Share 會開晒所有選項：X、Facebook、Reddit、WhatsApp、Threads、電郵同複製連結。
