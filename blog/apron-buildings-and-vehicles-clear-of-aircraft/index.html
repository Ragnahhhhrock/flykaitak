Fix: Buildings and Vehicles Clear of Aircraft

The car park and fire station moved out of the pushback lanes, and service vehicles wait out of sight.

3 October 20261 min readFly Kai Tak

Aircraft, buildings and service vehicles no longer overlap on the apron.

What is new

  • The car park and the fire station stood in the pushback lanes of the first stands. They now sit east of the stands, clear of every wing.
  • Baggage tractors, carts and catering trucks wait out of sight until their aircraft docks. They no longer sit where a departing aircraft is pushed back.
  • The spotters' roof view now looks along the runway from the new car park position.

What did not change

  • Traffic still follows the sim clock, so rewind and fast-forward still work.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.

停機坪上嘅飛機、建築物同地勤車輛而家唔會再互相重疊。

有咩新嘢

  • 停車場同消防局原本企喺頭幾個停機位嘅推機路線上。而家搬到停機位東面，避開晒所有機翼。
  • 行李拖車、貨車同餐飲車會喺視線以外等，直至飛機泊定先出現，唔會再企喺離港飛機推後嘅位置。
  • 觀機人士嘅停車場天台視角，而家由新位置沿跑道望出去。

冇變嘅嘢

  • 交通仍然跟模擬時鐘行，倒帶同快進照用。
  • 自動駕駛喺所有天氣預設都仍然可以降落，唔操作嘅話仍然會墜毀。
