Bay lines on the car park roof

White parking bay lines now mark out rows of 5 m by 2.5 m bays on the Plane Spotter roof.

The car park roof now has white parking bay lines painted across the deck.

Bays on the roof

Each bay is 5 m long and 2.5 m wide. One row runs against the far wall, and three back-to-back double rows sit beyond it with aisles between them. The runway side stays clear for the spotters, who stand behind the crowd barrier.

Try this

Choose Plane Spotter: car park roof in the view menu and look back across the deck.

停車場天台地面而家畫咗白色嘅停車位線。

天台停車位

每個車位 5 米長、2.5 米闊。遠邊牆腳有一排，之後有三組背對背嘅雙排車位，中間有車道。跑道嗰邊留空，畀企喺人群欄障後面嘅飛機迷用。

試吓

喺視角選單揀 Plane Spotter: car park roof，然後望返轉頭睇天台地面。
