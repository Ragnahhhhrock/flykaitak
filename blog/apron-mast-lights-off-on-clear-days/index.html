Apron mast lights off on clear days

The red floodlight masts now stay dark on a clear day and light up at night, in overcast weather and in fog.

5 October 20261 min readFly Kai Tak

The red floodlight masts over the stands no longer shine on a clear, sunny day. Their lamps and red obstruction lights now follow the same rule as the sim's other obstruction lights: on at night, and on by day only when the sky is overcast or foggy.

Start Plane Spotter with the weather on Clear, then again with Rain or Fog, and compare the masts.
