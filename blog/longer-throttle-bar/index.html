A longer thrust bar for finer control

The touch thrust bar is up to 260 px tall, so small thrust changes are easier to set.

4 October 20261 min readFly Kai Tak

The thrust lever bar on the touch controls is now about twice as long on a desktop-size screen, so each percent of thrust covers more pixels and small adjustments are easier.

260 pxLongest barwas 140 px

1%Thrust stepsunchanged

What changed

The thrust bar used to be a fixed 140 px tall. It now grows with the window, up to 260 px, and stays shorter on small screens so it never covers the altitude tape. On phones in landscape it is up to 170 px, up from 96 px. The thrust still moves in 1% steps and the autopilot still takes over the levers the same way.
