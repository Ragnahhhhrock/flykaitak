The ATC radio voices are now pre-recorded and play straight from the game's own files. A call no longer needs a live request to a voice service, so it starts at once, and the voices cannot run out of credits mid-flight.

What changed

  • Every call is built from fixed phrases plus numbers read out as digits ("one one eight decimal seven", "niner"), the way real ATC read-outs go. Callsigns now come from a set of 40, so every one is recorded.
  • About 320 short recordings cover every call the radio can make, on both runways, in every weather and in every game mode, including go-arounds and "check your height" calls.
  • The recordings are joined together in your browser, with the quiet gaps trimmed and a short pause at commas and full stops. The radio filter, static and squelch click are unchanged, and so are the captions.
  • The Approach, Tower, your readbacks and other crews each keep their own voice.

If a call is not in the recordings

  • The radio asks the voice service for that one piece, and if that is not available it uses the browser's speech voice, exactly as before.
  • R still turns the radio off, and Sound off mutes the voices.
  • On the helicopter tour the radio stays text only, so the guide is the only voice.

The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
