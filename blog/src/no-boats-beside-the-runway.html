The water around Kai Tak's runway is now empty. Junks, yachts, tugs, launches and police boats no longer moor or roam in Kowloon Bay or just off the strip, because there was no maritime traffic there under the approach.

What changed

  • The moored junks and yachts in Kowloon Bay are gone
  • Tugs, walla-walla launches and police boats no longer roam Kowloon Bay
  • Craft elsewhere in the harbour keep at least 900 m from the runway and its extended end
  • The junk that sat off the runway is gone; the Boat in the harbour view stays, low on the water at the same spot
  • Ships on the charted Victoria Harbour and Lei Yue Mun fairway still pass beyond the runway end
