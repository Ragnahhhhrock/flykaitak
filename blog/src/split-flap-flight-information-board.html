Kai Tak now has its flight information board: an old split-flap board that lists every arrival and departure. It sits on the home screen and in Plane Spotter, so you can see what is due in, what is on the stand and what has gone.

On the home screen

The board hangs over the live view behind the menu. It lists the arrivals, including the two jets in the hold and the demo aircraft on the approach (marked in orange), and the departures from the stands.

In Plane Spotter

Pick Plane Spotter or Spotter · Runway 31 and the board appears at the top right. It follows the six turnarounds and the aircraft in the hold. Press B, or the Flights button, to hide or show it. On a phone it starts hidden: open More and tap Flights.

What the board shows

  • Time: the arrival time for arrivals and the push back time for departures, on a Hong Kong clock (day starts at 10:20, night at 21:40).
  • Flight: a flight number with a colour square that matches the aircraft's tail. The airlines and numbers are made up, so no real airline appears.
  • From / To: where the flight is coming from or going to.
  • Stand: the stand number from the sim's apron.
  • Status: read from the aircraft itself. Arrivals go from Expected to On approach, Short final, Landed, Taxiing and At stand. Departures go from Boarding to Pushback, Taxiing, Holding, Take-off and Departed.

Every letter is its own flap, and a flap flips when its letter changes. Finished flights dim and drop to the bottom of their list.

In Cantonese

The board follows the EN / 粵 button on the home screen and shows its headings, cities and status in Cantonese.

Try this

Start Plane Spotter, watch a flight go from Boarding to Pushback on the board, then switch to the Exterior view and follow it out to the runway.
