Replay videos now have sound, and the radio calls are on screen. There's also a new Get share link button: it puts your replay on its own page, so a link posted to X, Facebook or WhatsApp shows your result and plays the video.

A sound track

The sound is made from the same sound design you hear in flight. It's rendered alongside the video, frame by frame, so it matches what's on screen:

  • Engines and turbine whine that follow the thrust, wind that rises with speed, and rain in wet weather.
  • Gear rumble, reverse thrust, tyre squeal on the rollout, the thump of touchdown and, when it goes wrong, the crash.
  • The mix follows the camera. On a fly-by the jet is faint in the distance, then loud as it passes. From the cockpit, the sound is muffled.
  • The report card at the end gets a two-tone airport chime.

Radio calls

Tower and approach calls, and your read-backs, now appear in the replay and in the video, colour-coded like the in-flight radio. In the video each call opens and closes with the squelch click, with static underneath. The browser's spoken voices can't be recorded, so the words are captions.

Share links

After you make a video, Get share link uploads it and gives you a link to a page with your video, your result and a Fly it yourself button. The page carries video tags, so the link previews with your report card on X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Reddit and Threads. Buttons for each are right under the link.

Share links are switched on separately from the sim, so the button appears only once the replay service is running. Download and the phone's share sheet work without it.

Uploads are limited to 12 an hour, and the pages are kept out of search engines. Nothing about you is stored with a replay, just the video and the result on the report card.
