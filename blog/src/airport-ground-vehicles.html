The apron is no longer empty. Vehicles now serve the six parked aircraft, and a fire station and cargo shed stand beside the runway.

What is new

  • Buses pull up to the front door and wait, then drive back to the depot.
  • Catering trucks drive to the stand and lift their box up to the door.
  • Baggage tractors tow three loaded carts under the wing.
  • Container and box freight trucks loop around the cargo shed, and a lorry shuttles along the service road.
  • The fire station holds crash tenders and a rescue vehicle. One tender drives out to a standby point and back.
  • At night the vehicles show headlights, tail lamps and beacons.

What did not change

  • The vehicles run on the sim clock, so rewind and fast-forward still work.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
