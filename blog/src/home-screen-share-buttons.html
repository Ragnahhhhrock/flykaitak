The home screen now has share buttons, so you can send Fly Kai Tak to a friend without leaving the menu.

Share from the home screen

Under the demo reel there is a new Share Fly Kai Tak row: X, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp, Threads, Email and Copy link. Each one opens with a short line about the game and a link to flykaitak.com. On phones and browsers that support it, a Share… button opens the system share sheet too.

The text and the button labels follow the EN / 粵 button, so the message goes out in English or Cantonese. Each link carries a different utm_source, so shares are counted by network.

Try this

Open the home screen, press Copy link and paste it into a chat. After a flight, the report card has its own share buttons too.
