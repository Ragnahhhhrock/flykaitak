Highlights, straight away

There's no button to press. Land, crash or go around, and the replay starts on its own with the cut of the flight that matters: the opening seconds (or lift-off), the checkerboard and the 47° turn over Kowloon City, touchdown, and the crash if there was one. A diamond on the timeline marks each moment. When the highlights finish, the report card comes up. If you touch the controls, the replay stays open until you press Report card.

Save it as a video

Save video is in the replay bar, and there's a Video button on the report card. Pick a format and press Make video. The sim renders the highlights frame by frame at 30 frames a second, so the video is smooth even if your device isn't fast. A progress bar shows how far it has got, and you can cancel.

  • Landscape 16:9, 1280×720: for YouTube, X and Facebook.
  • Portrait 9:16, 720×1280: for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. The camera widens so the wings still fit.

What's in the video

The video opens with a title, then plays the highlights with a REPLAY tag, captions for each moment, your altitude and speed, the aircraft and route, flykaitak.com and a checkerboard band along the bottom. It ends on your report card and an invitation to try it.

Share it

  • Download MP4 saves the file.
  • Share… on a phone opens the share sheet with the video attached, so it goes straight to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp or anywhere else.
  • Copy caption copies your score line and a link to flykaitak.com.
  • Links open YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, ready for you to add the downloaded video to a new post.

Keys in the replay: Space play/pause, ← → 5 seconds back or forward, H highlights or full flight, C camera, S save video, Esc report card.

Everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Chrome, Edge and Safari make H.264 video. Other browsers fall back to VP9 where they can. The video has no sound yet. Engine and radio audio are next.
