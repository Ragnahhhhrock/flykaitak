On the home screen, the Share button sat about 15 pixels lower than the Facebook and Instagram buttons beside it. It now lines up with them on the same row.

What changed

  • Share, Facebook and Instagram now share one top edge and the same 40-pixel height.
  • The cause was a spacing rule meant for the Controls panel that also applied to the Share menu. It is switched off there.
  • Share still opens the same menu of X, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp, Threads, email and copy link.
