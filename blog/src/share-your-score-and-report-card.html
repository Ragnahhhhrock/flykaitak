When a flight ends, the result screen now has a row of share buttons. Post your grade and score to X, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp or Threads, send it by email, copy the text, or save the report card as an image to post anywhere.

What you can share

  • X, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp, Threads. Each opens that site's post window with your result already written, for example the aircraft, grade, score, touchdown rate and rank, and a link to flykaitak.com.
  • Email. Opens a new message with the same text.
  • Copy. Puts the text and link on your clipboard.
  • Save image. Downloads the report card as a 1200 x 630 PNG, the size social sites show best.
  • Share… On phones and other devices with built-in sharing, this sends the report card image and text straight to any app you choose.

The report card

The image carries your grade, score out of 100, your high score rank and callsign (for hand-flown IGS 13 landings), the touchdown stats, the aircraft, time of day and weather, all on the Kai Tak checkerboard. A crash or an abandoned approach gets a card too, so you can share those as well.

Tip. Text and button labels follow the EN / 粵 toggle on the home screen, so the share text can go out in Cantonese too.
