The Fly Kai Tak high score table has its first perfect 100. A pilot flew the IGS 13 approach by hand and scored full marks for touchdown rate, touchdown point, centreline, bank and airspeed, which is a Captain rank with nothing left to improve.

There is one catch: the name box was left empty, so the landing sits on the table as PILOT. Whoever you are, well flown. Come back and claim it.

Why the name was missed

The callsign box was a small field with a quiet label, and a landing is added to the table the moment you touch down. If you were looking at the grade, the table or the Fly it again button, it was easy not to see that the box was waiting for you.

A clearer name box

The result screen now asks for your name outright.

  • Enter your name is the label, with a Save button beside the box. Type your name and press Enter or Save.
  • If no name is saved yet, a line above the box says your score is on the table with no name, the box is outlined in orange and pulses a few times, and on a computer the cursor is already in it.
  • On a phone or tablet the box is highlighted but not focused, so the keyboard does not cover your result.
  • Once you save, the screen confirms the name and the table updates straight away.
  • Leaving the box empty no longer saves PILOT as your name, so you are asked again next time.

Chasing the 100

Only hand-flown landings in the IGS 13 approach mode are ranked, so the autopilot does not count. The score is out of 100 for touchdown rate, touchdown point, centreline, bank and airspeed. 90 and above is Captain.

Your name is remembered in your browser, so you only type it once. You can change it on any later result screen.
