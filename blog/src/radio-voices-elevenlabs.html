The ATC radio can now speak with studio voices from ElevenLabs instead of the browser's built-in speech engine. Approach, Tower, your readbacks and other crews each get their own voice.

What changed

  • Hong Kong Approach 119.1, Kai Tak Tower 118.7, your readbacks and the other crews on frequency are each voiced separately.
  • The voice is played through a narrow radio band with a little static under the words, then the usual squelch click when the call ends.
  • Calls still follow your position and the live weather, and the captions on the left of the screen are unchanged.
  • Each line is generated once and kept, so the same call is not generated twice. The key stays on the server and never reaches your browser.

If the voices are not available

  • The sim falls back to the browser's speech voice, exactly as before. Nothing is lost and the radio is never silent because of it.
  • R still turns the radio off, and Sound off mutes the voices.
  • On the helicopter tour the radio stays text only, so the guide is the only voice.

The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
