Kai Tak now has a cargo terminal and freighters, working alongside the passenger flights.

The cargo apron

The cargo terminal stands at the north-east end of the apron, as on the 1996 chart: maroon walls, white end towers, and stacked containers on the roof. Stands 41 to 46 lie on the far side of the lane, with each freighter nose-in to a roller door.

Freighters in the schedule

A 747-400F and an A300-600F join the traffic, in two cargo airlines' colours. Two of the eight runway slots in each cycle are now cargo flights: they land, taxi to a cargo stand, are unloaded, and later push back and depart. The flight board shows them as Loading.

Cargo handling vehicles

At each cargo stand a main-deck loader docks at the side door, and a tug brings a train of container dollies. A forklift shuttles pallets along the terminal's airside docks, and container trucks work the landside docks. They run on the sim clock, like the other ground vehicles.

Maintenance hangars and car park

The maintenance hangars are in the far north-west corner behind the passenger terminal, with open door bays and skylight strips, and aircraft in for maintenance on the apron in front. The car park roof now has parked cars in its bays and a bigger crowd of spotters.
