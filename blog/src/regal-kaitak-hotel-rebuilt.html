The hotel across Prince Edward Road East from the car park has been rebuilt from photos and a roof plan of the real building, the Regal Kai Tak Hotel. It now carries its own name, 富豪啟德酒店, down the end wall and REGAL KAITAK HOTEL across the podium.

The shape

  • Plan: a long slab along the road with a wide wing running back to Sa Po Road at its north-east end, and both ends cut at an angle, as on the roof plan.
  • Tower: continuous bands of dark tinted glass and brown spandrels on every long face, solid brick end walls with a column of small windows, and a deep brown cornice.
  • Podium: brick, with a dark glazed band over the shopfronts, the hotel name across the road face and a planted terrace along the road.
  • Roof: flat, with plant units and a round tank.

The footbridge and the night

The covered footbridge now meets the podium near its north-east end, as in the photos. At night the rooms light up at random and the name on the end wall and the podium glows.

Sources: photos and a roof plan of the Regal Kai Tak Hotel, and Gwulo's page on the Kai Tak Airport Hotel Footbridge.
