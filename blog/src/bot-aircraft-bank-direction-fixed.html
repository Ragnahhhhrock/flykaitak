Other aircraft in the sim were banking the wrong way. A jet turning right showed its left wing down, and the same for left turns. Arriving and departing traffic now rolls into the turn like it should.

What changed

Arrivals bank into the 47° right turn over Kowloon City, and departures bank into their right turn over the harbour. Both were drawn with the bank reversed. The holding jets already banked correctly. We fixed the two that did not.

What did not change

  • Your own aircraft was always drawn correctly. Only the other traffic was wrong.
  • The aileron movement on the other jets now matches the bank too.
  • Flying with no input still ends in a crash, and the autopilot still lands in every weather preset.
