Plane spotters never forgot the days a Concorde came to Kai Tak. It was a rare visit, so in Plane Spotter it is a special: a supersonic delta-wing jet that lands, parks and leaves again.

The visit

Once an hour the special arrives on the active runway, taxis to stand 6 and, about ten minutes later, pushes back and departs. The usual turnaround at that stand skips that slot so nothing is in the way. A toast tells you when it is on approach.

The shape

The model has a slender fuselage, a long ogival delta wing with elevons, four engines in long pods under it and a tall fin. The nose droops for landing and taxi and rises for flight. It lands nose-high and fast, and it climbs steeply after take-off.

On the board

The flight information board shows its arrival and departure as special rows with a gold outline. The airline is made up: no real names or liveries.

Try this

Choose Spotter · Concorde special on the home screen to have the visit start straight away. It is Plane Spotter only; you cannot fly it.
