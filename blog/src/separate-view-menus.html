The single camera menu is now three: Flight views, Plane spotter and Terminal interior. Each list is shorter and easier to scan.

What is in each menu

  • Flight views: Cockpit, Exterior, Tower and Cabin seat
  • Plane spotter: Kowloon City street, Prince Edward Road, car park roof, boat in the harbour, top of Checkerboard Hill, Lei Yue Mun (Runway 31) and The Peak
  • Terminal interior: departures hall, departure immigration, shop arcade, gate lounge, ground floor and stairs, arrivals hall and front entrance

Picking a view in one menu resets the other two to their titles, so you can always see which one is active. On narrow screens the Plane spotter and Terminal interior menus sit under More.
