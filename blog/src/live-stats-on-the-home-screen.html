How it works

  • The numbers come from the same anonymous counts as flykaitak.com/stats. Nothing about you is shown.
  • The page ships with recent totals, so the strip never sits empty. It asks for the live totals as it loads and counts on to them when they arrive.
  • The tiles slide in one after another and a green dot marks the feed as live. With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, the numbers appear at once.
  • The labels switch with the EN / 粵 button like the rest of the home screen.
