What changed

The home screen already shows the sim running behind the menu: a 747 on the IGS approach, with the other airliners around the airport. The map now draws that same flight and traffic in the top left corner, so you can see where everything is before you start. Press M to hide it and press it again to bring it back.

The Map with live aircraft box in Options follows the key, and ticking it by hand does the same thing. Whatever it says when you press Begin descent is how the flight starts.
