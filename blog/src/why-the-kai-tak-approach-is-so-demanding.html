Runway 13 at Kai Tak ends in Kowloon Bay, and the way to it runs right over the city. In Fly Kai Tak you fly the IGS 13 approach: you start at about 3,100 ft over Tsing Yi, heading 088, and every run ends in a landing, which is graded, or a crash, which comes with its cause.

One leg, then a turn

The first part is simple: fly toward the checkerboard, the orange-and-white marker on the hill at Kowloon Tsai. The difficulty is what comes next.

The turn is low and close

You make a 47° right turn low over Kowloon City. You roll in at about 650 ft, 2 NM from touchdown, and roll out at about 140 ft. That leaves very little straight final to fix a mistake, so the turn has to be set up before you start it.

Hills on one side, buildings below

Lion Rock and the ridge behind Kowloon stay off the left wing on the approach, and the streets below are packed with buildings. The sim keeps its housing estates and billboards under the approach clearance, but you are still flying close to a lot of roofs.

Weather and a short runway end

Wind, gusts and turbulence change the flight, and the sim has clear, rain, typhoon, storm, low cloud and fog presets, plus day, dusk and night. After the turn you land on a runway whose far end is the water of the bay.

Learn it in steps. The Landing lesson walks you through the approach one step at a time, with guide hoops and live meters. When you are ready, turn the hoops off and fly it by hand.
