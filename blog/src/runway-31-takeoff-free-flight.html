You can now take off from Runway 31 as well as land on it. Free flight has a second starting point at the Kowloon Bay end of the runway, pointing at Kowloon City.

Choose Runway 31 take-off

On the setup screen, pick Runway 31 take-off next to Free flight. You start lined up on Runway 31, and Tower clears you for take-off on that runway. Push the thrust to full, pull back at about 150 kt and raise the gear. Kowloon City is dead ahead, so climb and turn left.

The same airport, mirrored

The other aircraft follow Runway 31 too: departures line up and take off to the north-west, arrivals land from the east, and the holding aircraft move to the south-east. Radio calls and chatter use Runway 31 as well. Landings in free flight are still graded, and you can carry on with a touch-and-go.
