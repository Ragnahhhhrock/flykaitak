Boats in the harbour come right up to the seawall around Kai Tak's peninsula again, passing alongside the edges of Runway 13/31 as they did in the 1990s. The one place they never go is the water inlet under the taxiways to the south apron.

What changed

  • Junks and yachts moor along the shore in Kowloon Bay, and tugs, launches and police boats roam there, close to the seawall
  • The junk moored off the runway is back, and so is the Boat in the harbour view from it
  • No boat is moored in, or routed through, the inlet between the runway-side taxiway and the south apron
