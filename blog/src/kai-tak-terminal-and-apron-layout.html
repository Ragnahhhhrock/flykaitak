The terminal used to run alongside the runway. On the real airport it did not: the passenger terminal stood at the north-west end of the apron and its pier ran out at right angles to the runway. The sim now follows the airport chart and the terminal plans.

What moved

The pier now sits at the head of the apron with its face toward the runway end, the main terminal block and the multi-storey car park beyond its west end, and two maintenance hangars across the apron. The tower stands on its own base building in the middle of the apron. Stand positions were measured from the chart, so stands 1 to 8 are spaced 63 to 87 m apart rather than evenly.

Apron lanes and stands

Three lanes leave taxiway A at right angles. D3 runs along the stands, and D2 and D1 serve rows of remote stands (numbered 11 to 19). The yellow guidance lines, nose-wheel stop bars and red stand boxes were redrawn for the new layout, with the stand number painted at the lead-in.

Traffic follows the new routes

Arrivals exit onto taxiway A, turn into lane D3 and then onto the stand's lead-in line. Departures are pushed straight back by the tug, turned a quarter circle toward taxiway A and taxi out from there. Catering trucks, baggage tractors and the pushback tugs run between the wingtips at each stand, and the flight board shows the stand numbers 1 to 6. Stands 7 and 8 always hold a parked aircraft.

Landings are unaffected: the autopilot still lands on both runways in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
