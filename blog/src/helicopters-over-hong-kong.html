Helicopters now work Hong Kong's skies in the sim. The Government Flying Service flies from its pads at Kai Tak, a Macau shuttle runs from Shun Tak, and a hotel helicopter tours the harbour.

What is new

  • Two helipads on the Kai Tak south apron, beside the flight services building, with a Black Hawk and an S-76 that lift off, fly a sortie over Kowloon Bay and come back. Two more helicopters stand parked beside the building.
  • Shun Tak Heliport at Sheung Wan, on the roof of the Macau ferry terminal. Two S-76 take turns: one leaves for the west past Lantau while the other arrives.
  • A helipad on the roof of the Peninsula Hotel tower in Tsim Sha Tsui, with a helicopter that flies a sightseeing loop over the harbour.
  • The rotors spin up before lift-off and spin down after landing. Wheels fold away on the S-76. Lights show at night.
  • Helicopters appear on the map as round blue dots. The shuttle and hotel flights stay on the ground in a typhoon, a storm, fog or low cloud, and the Government Flying Service stays on the ground in a typhoon.

What the research found

  • The Government Flying Service (GFS) moved into new premises on the Kai Tak south apron from August 1992 and took over from the Royal Hong Kong Auxiliary Air Force on 1 April 1993. Its 1997 fleet was 6 S-76 and 3 S-70 helicopters and 2 King Air aeroplanes. It moved to Chek Lap Kok with the new airport in 1998.
  • The Macau shuttle began in November 1990 with Bell 222 helicopters. Three S-76C replaced them from 1997. Its Hong Kong pad, on the ferry terminal at Shun Tak Centre, was built in 1986.
  • The Peninsula's rooftop helipad was certified in 1994.
  • The RAF Wessex of 28 Squadron left Kai Tak when the squadron disbanded in June 1997, so none are in the sim.

The full notes and sources are in docs/helicopters.md in the repository.

What is made up

  • Liveries, flight routes and times. The shuttle goes out and back in 1,300 seconds of sim time, and helicopters vanish when they are 30 km away.
  • The height of the Shun Tak pad.

What did not change

  • The helicopters run on the sim clock, so rewind and fast-forward still work.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
