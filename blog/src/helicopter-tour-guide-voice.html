The Hong Kong harbour helicopter scenic tour now has a voice. The guide reads each caption aloud as it comes up, in English or Cantonese.

What is new

  • Each of the 12 tour captions is spoken when it appears, from "Welcome aboard" on the Peninsula roof to the landing.
  • The guide uses a British English voice, a woman's where your browser has one, so it does not sound like the pilots on the radio.
  • Switch the home screen to 粵 and the guide speaks Cantonese, if your device has a Hong Kong Cantonese voice. Without one, the Cantonese captions stay on screen, silent.
  • The rotor and turbine noise drops while the guide talks and comes back up after.
  • Three small bars on the left of the caption pulse while the guide is speaking.

How it behaves

  • The voice uses your browser's built-in speech, so how it sounds depends on your device.
  • The guide only speaks at normal time. Speed up time and the voice stops; the captions carry on.
  • Rewind into the middle of a caption and the guide stays quiet until the next one starts. Pause also pauses the voice.
  • The Sound button turns the guide off with the rest of the audio.

What did not change

  • The route, captions and timing are the same.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
