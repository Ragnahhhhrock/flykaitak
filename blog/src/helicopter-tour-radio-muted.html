On the Hong Kong harbour helicopter scenic tour, the guide is now the only voice you hear. Radio traffic is no longer spoken.

What changed

  • The Approach and Tower calls to the jets landing at Kai Tak are silent on the tour. No voices and no squelch clicks.
  • With Radio on, the calls still show as text on the left of the screen, so you can follow the traffic across the bay.
  • The guide's commentary is no longer held up or cut off by radio calls.
  • The rotor and turbine sound and the weather stay as they were.

What did not change

  • In Plane Spotter and every flying mode, the radio is spoken as before.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
