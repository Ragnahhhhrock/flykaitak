The corner map no longer switches itself on when the home screen loads. It starts hidden, and you turn it on when you want it.

What changed

The map used to be on by default. It is now off until you ask for it, on the home screen and in flights. Press M, or tick Map with live aircraft under Options, to show it. The tick box starts unticked and follows the key, and whatever it says when you press Begin descent is how the flight starts.

Once on, the map keeps its larger size on the home screen and in Plane Spotter, and moves clear of High scores.
