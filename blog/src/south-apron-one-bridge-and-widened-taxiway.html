The airfield east of the runway now follows the 1990s Kai Tak plan more closely: the flight apron runs straight on into the south apron, only one bridge crosses the inlet, and the taxiway tarmac widens toward the Runway 31 end.

What is new

  • The apron tarmac continues from the main apron into the south apron, with no water between them. Tarmac now closes the head of the inlet beside the runway.
  • Taxiway B4 runs the full length of the south apron, starting where it meets the main apron.
  • The inlet has a single bridge, near its far end, linking the runway-side taxiway to the south apron. The second bridge is gone.
  • Toward the Runway 31 end, the tarmac beside the parallel taxiway widens out over the water, tapering in at both ends.
  • Taxiing aircraft can use all the new tarmac; the grass and the water stay off limits.

What did not change

  • Stands 71-81, the helipads and the flight services building stay where they were on the south apron.
  • The autopilot still lands in every weather preset, and flying with no input still ends in a crash.
